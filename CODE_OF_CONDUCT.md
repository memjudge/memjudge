# Code of Conduct

MemJudge adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique work rather than people, and do not
harass anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any MemJudge space.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **admin@memjudge.org**. Reports are handled confidentially
by the maintainers, who may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, following
the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
